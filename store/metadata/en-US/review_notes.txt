Nof1 is a self-tracking app for interventions selected and entered by the user. It does not contain a treatment library, calculate doses, recommend interventions, or connect to HealthKit in this release.

Review path:
1. Create an account with an email address available to the reviewer, or use the review account entered in App Store Connect.
2. On Daily, record quick metrics or tap Log Entry to enter a user-chosen intervention.
3. Open Profile > My Experiments > New Experiment to create a structured experiment.
4. Open Profile > Subscription to view the RevenueCat paywall and Restore Purchases action.
5. Open Profile > Delete Account to permanently delete the signed-in account and app-owned data.
6. Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, support, JSON export, and diagnostic-log export are available in Profile.

The App Store subscription must be tested with Apple's sandbox environment. No external purchasing link is shown in the iOS app.
