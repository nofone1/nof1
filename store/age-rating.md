# Age-rating and content notes

Nof1 requires users to be 18 or older in its Terms. The release does not ship
graphic medical content, drug or treatment recommendations, dose calculators,
gambling, violence, sexual content, unrestricted web access, or social features.

Complete Apple's current questionnaire truthfully. Where the questionnaire asks
about medical or wellness information, disclose that Nof1 records user-entered
wellness observations but is not a medical device and does not provide medical
advice. Do not select an answer merely to achieve a particular rating.
