# Privacy Policy

Effective September 1, 2026

Nof1 processes the information needed to provide its private self-tracking service. This policy describes what we process, why we process it, and the choices available to you.

## Information we process

We process account information such as your name, email address, and account identifier; content you choose to enter, including experiments, routines, intervention logs, metrics, notes, schedules, and saved items; App Store subscription identifiers and entitlement status; and technical logs needed for security, reliability, and support.

## How we use information

We use this information to authenticate your account, sync and display your records, organize your entries, manage subscription access, provide support, prevent abuse, and maintain the reliability of the service. We do not sell personal information or use sensitive information for targeted advertising.

## Service providers

We use Clerk for authentication, Convex for synced application data, RevenueCat for subscription entitlement management, and Apple for App Store purchases. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy commitments. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect users, the service, or others.

## Storage and retention

Some settings and cached records are stored on your device. Signed-in records may be stored in our cloud systems so they can sync across devices. We retain account content while your account is active and delete app-owned account data when you use in-app account deletion, subject to limited retention required for security, legal, tax, dispute, and transaction obligations. Apple and payment service providers may retain transaction records independently.

## Your choices

You can edit or delete individual records, export a JSON copy of locally available app data, and permanently delete your Nof1 account from Profile in the app. Account deletion does not cancel an App Store subscription; subscriptions are managed through your Apple account. You may also request access, correction, or deletion by contacting us.

## Security and international processing

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no storage or transmission system is completely secure. Providers may process information in the United States and other countries subject to their safeguards and applicable law.

## Age

Nof1 is intended only for adults age 18 or older and is not directed to children.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will identify changes by a new effective date. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to [anam@revyl.ai](mailto:anam@revyl.ai).
