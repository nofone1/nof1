# Support

For account, billing, privacy, or technical help, email [anam@revyl.ai](mailto:anam@revyl.ai).

## Delete your account

Open Nof1, sign in, and go to **Profile > Delete Account**. Confirming deletion permanently removes the signed-in identity, Nof1-owned cloud records, and local app data. This cannot be undone.

Deleting an account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions in **Profile > Subscription** or in your Apple account subscription settings.

If you cannot access the app, email us from the address associated with the account. We will verify the request before deleting account data.
